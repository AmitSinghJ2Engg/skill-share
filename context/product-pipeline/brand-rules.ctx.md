# Brand Rules -- Ismokraft

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Ismokraft
**Tagline:** Premium Indian Woodcraft
**Focus:** Functional and decorative wooden products for Indian and global consumers

## Core Values

- **Craftsmanship:** Every product reflects skilled artisan work from India's woodworking traditions
- **Quality materials:** Wood dominance >= 70% by volume in every product
- **Personalization:** Engraving and customization capability is a key differentiator
- **Sustainability:** Natural materials, minimal packaging waste, support for artisan communities

## Material Standards

- Primary material: wood (sheesham, mango wood, teak, rosewood, bamboo)
- Material dominance: >= 70% wood by volume
- Accent materials allowed: brass, iron, glass, leather (< 30%)
- Manufacturing clusters: Jodhpur (primary), Moradabad (metal accents), Vrindavan (religious items)
- Country of Origin: India (mandatory on all listings and packaging)

## Pricing Rules

- Price floor: 1000 INR (no products below this price)
- Sweet spot: 800-2000 INR
- Positioning: mid-premium segment (above mass-market, below luxury imports)

## Target Customer Profiles

1. **Workspace professionals** (25-40, urban, desk accessories and organizers)
2. **Gift shoppers** (25-45, occasions like birthdays, anniversaries, Diwali, weddings)
3. **Home decorators** (25-45, women-skewed, wall and table decor)
4. **Religious households** (30-60, puja mandirs and temple accessories)
5. **Hobbyists and collectors** (25-50, chess boards, display cases, specialty items)

## Tone of Voice

- Warm and approachable, not corporate
- Emphasize craftsmanship and Indian heritage
- Use descriptive product language (handcrafted, artisan, premium wood)
- Avoid: discount language, mass-market terms, comparison to cheap alternatives
- Product descriptions should evoke the experience of owning the product

## Listing Standards

- All product titles include "wooden" or specific wood type
- Feature images show the product in a lifestyle setting (not just product-on-white)
- A+ content (where available) tells the Ismokraft story
- Reviews and Q&A are monitored and responded to within 24 hours
