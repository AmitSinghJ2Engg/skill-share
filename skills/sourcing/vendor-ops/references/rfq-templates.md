# RFQ Templates — RFQ Generator

**Purpose:** RFQ formats by vendor type. Read at Step 2.

---

## Template A — Factory / Private Label Vendor

```
Subject: Request for Quotation — [PRODUCT_NAME] — Private Label

Dear [VENDOR_NAME] Team,

We are Ismokraft, an ecommerce brand selling on Amazon India and our own Shopify store.
We are evaluating suppliers for [PRODUCT_NAME] in the [CATEGORY] category.

We are looking for a long-term manufacturing partner for private label production.

PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:
• Product: [PRODUCT_NAME]
• Material: [MATERIAL / TO BE DISCUSSED]
• Dimensions: [DIMENSIONS / TO BE DISCUSSED]
• Weight: [WEIGHT_GRAMS]g approx.
• Finish/Colour: [FINISH / TO BE DISCUSSED]
• Packaging: [PACKAGING / Standard brown box]
• Certifications required: [CERTIFICATIONS / None specified]
• Delivery location: [DELIVERY_LOCATION]

QUOTATION REQUESTED:
• Target unit price: [TARGET_PRICE_RANGE] (at [TARGET_MOQ] units)
• MOQ for first order: [TARGET_MOQ] units
• Lead time: please specify from PO to dispatch
• Sample availability: Yes / No, and sample cost

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
We will evaluate based on price, quality, lead time, and responsiveness.
We intend to place a trial order within 30 days of vendor selection.

Please share your product catalogue, GSTIN, and company profile along with the quote.

Looking forward to your response.

Best regards,
Ismokraft Sourcing Team
[CONTACT]
```

## Template B — White Label / Ready-to-Ship Vendor

```
Subject: White Label Inquiry — [PRODUCT_NAME]

Dear [VENDOR_NAME] Team,

We are Ismokraft, an ecommerce brand on Amazon India. We are interested in your
[PRODUCT_NAME] for white label supply under our brand.

PRODUCT INTEREST:
• Product: [PRODUCT_NAME]
• Category: [CATEGORY]
• Packaging: Our own packaging / label to be applied by vendor
• Delivery: [DELIVERY_LOCATION]

QUOTATION REQUESTED:
• Price per unit at [TARGET_MOQ] minimum order
• Minimum order quantity for first purchase
• Lead time from order to delivery
• Sample availability and cost

Please also confirm:
• Can you print/apply our brand label?
• Do you supply GSTINs on all invoices?
• What quality guarantee or return policy applies?

We are ready to proceed quickly if terms are suitable.

Best regards,
Ismokraft
[CONTACT]
```

## Template C — Dropship / RTS (Ready to Ship)

```
Subject: Dropship / Reseller Inquiry — [PRODUCT_NAME]

Dear [VENDOR_NAME] Team,

We are Ismokraft, an ecommerce brand on Amazon India and Shopify. We are interested
in a dropship or reseller arrangement for [PRODUCT_NAME].

INQUIRY DETAILS:
• Product: [PRODUCT_NAME]
• Category: [CATEGORY]
• Expected monthly volumes: [TARGET_MOQ] units/month initially
• Delivery model: Dropship to end customer / Bulk to our warehouse

QUOTATION REQUESTED:
• Price per unit at 100 / 500 / 1000+ units
• Dropship per-order fee (if applicable)
• Stock availability and lead time
• Return policy for damaged / unsold stock

Please share your catalogue and GST invoice terms.

Best regards,
Ismokraft
[CONTACT]
```

## Tone Guidelines (from ism-business-authority)

- Professional and direct. No filler language.
- Relationship-first tone — we are looking for long-term partners, not one-off transactions.
- Specific about quantities and timeline — vendors respond faster to concrete asks.
- Always mention Amazon India and ecommerce context — this signals legitimate volume buyer.
- Always request GSTIN compliance — non-GST vendors are disqualifying for our model.
- State "trial order within 30 days" for factory vendors — creates urgency.
