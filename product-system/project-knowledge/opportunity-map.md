# Ismokraft Wood Opportunity Map

7 zones of wooden product categories. Each zone defines what to research, when, and where it's manufactured.

## Zones

### Zone 1 — Workspace Products (P1, 3x/week: Mon, Fri)
Target: Working professionals, WFH setup. Cluster: Jodhpur. Price: 800–3000 INR.
Seed keywords: wooden desk organizer, wooden laptop stand, wooden cable management, wooden pen holder, wooden monitor riser.
Sub-categories: desk organization, laptop accessories, cable management, pen storage, monitor accessories.
Notes: High repeat potential. Premium wood finishes differentiate. No seasonal peak.

### Zone 2 — Personalized Gifts (P1, 3x/week: Tue)
Target: Gift shoppers, corporate buyers. Cluster: Jodhpur. Price: 800–4000 INR.
Seed keywords: personalized wooden gift, engraved wooden box, custom wooden photo frame, wooden anniversary gift, wooden name plate.
Sub-categories: anniversary gifting, corporate gifting, birthday gifting, keepsake, photo frames.
Notes: Personalization adds defensible margin. Engraving capability critical. Peak: Oct–Feb (festival + wedding season).

### Zone 3 — Home Decor (P2, 2x/week: Wed)
Target: Home decorators, interior design enthusiasts. Cluster: Jodhpur. Price: 600–3500 INR.
Seed keywords: wooden wall decor, wooden candle holder, wooden shelf display, wooden table decor, wooden tray.
Sub-categories: wall decor, table decor, candle display, shelf display, serving trays.
Notes: Weight ceiling critical — fragility risk with FBA shipping. Peak: Aug–Nov (festive home refresh).

### Zone 4 — Cultural Gifting (P2, 2x/week: Thu)
Target: Religious households, festival shoppers, NRI buyers. Cluster: Vrindavan. Price: 800–5000 INR.
Seed keywords: wooden puja mandir, wooden temple home, wooden god idol stand, wooden diya holder, wooden incense holder.
Sub-categories: puja items, temple furniture, festival gifting, deity display, incense holders.
Notes: Seasonal demand spikes 3–5x. Plan inventory early. Vrindavan cluster specializes. Peak: Aug–Nov (Navratri, Diwali, Karwa Chauth).

### Zone 5 — Jewelry & Accessories Storage (P3, 1x/week: Sat)
Target: Women 25–45, gifters. Cluster: Jodhpur. Price: 800–3000 INR.
Seed keywords: wooden jewelry box, wooden watch organizer, wooden earring stand, wooden ring holder, wooden bangle stand.
Sub-categories: ring storage, watch display, earring organization, bangle storage, jewelry boxes.
Notes: Personalization opportunity high. Velvet lining differentiates. Peak: Oct–Feb (gifting season).

### Zone 6 — Lifestyle Accessories (P3, 1x/week: Sun)
Target: Urban households, men 25–40. Cluster: Jodhpur. Price: 600–2500 INR.
Seed keywords: wooden key holder wall, wooden charging station, wooden remote organizer, wooden coaster set, wooden valet tray.
Sub-categories: key organization, charging accessories, remote storage, coasters, valet trays.
Notes: Functional + aesthetic. Everyday utility drives repeat purchase. No seasonal peak.

### Zone 7 — Hobby & Specialty Accessories (P4, 1x/month: 1st and 15th)
Target: Hobbyists, collectors, enthusiasts. Cluster: Jodhpur. Price: 1000–5000 INR.
Seed keywords: wooden chess board, wooden art supplies box, wooden guitar stand, wooden book ends, wooden display case.
Sub-categories: chess/gaming, art supplies, music accessories, book display, collectible display.
Notes: Niche but high-margin. Lower competition. No seasonal peak.

## Excluded Categories

toys, medical, electronics, apparel, baby, kids, licensed IP, food, beverages, supplements, cosmetics, pharmaceutical.

## Material Signals

wood, wooden, bamboo, timber, hardwood, sheesham, rosewood, teak, mango wood, acacia, walnut, pine, plywood.

## Manufacturing Clusters

Jodhpur: Primary wood cluster — handles most zones. Moradabad: Metal accents and mixed-material. Vrindavan: Religious/cultural items (Zone 4).
