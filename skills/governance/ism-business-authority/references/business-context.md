- **Time scarcity** — supplier outreach, packaging, listing creation, system building all compete for the same person's hours
- **Learning curve** — first product launch, first FBA shipment, first listing. Mistakes are expected. Processes should account for this.
- **System-building happening alongside launch** — the Zoho stack, artifacts, and processes are being built while trying to launch. Don't let system-building delay the launch.
